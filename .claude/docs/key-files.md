| `components/Timeline/AudioBedBar.jsx` | The A1 bed drawn as one bar, with the three spatial states that are the only thing the user can't otherwise see: **shorter** than the V1 sequence (bar + hatched "silence" remainder), **longer** (clipped at the sequence edge + a red cut marker), **equal** (flush). `EPS = 0.017` (1 frame at 60fps) keeps rounding dust out of "shorter", and the states are measured against `availSec = sequenceSec - startSec` (a 2 s bed under a 3 s sequence with a 1 s head hold is **exact**, not short). **Linked to V1 twice over.** Geometrically: it takes `clips` + `gapPx` and measures every edge through `sequencePosToPx`, so it sits under V1's actual pixels rather than over a continuous `sequenceSec * pps` span that drifts 2px per clip boundary.
